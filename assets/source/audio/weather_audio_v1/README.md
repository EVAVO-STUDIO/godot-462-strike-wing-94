# Weather sound candidates

Three original 16-second stereo loops were generated with EVAVO Audio Studio's clean-room `dos_tracker_pcm` profile and shooter role context: rain, storm rain and mountain wind. Generation receipts retain exact prompts, seeds, profile and waveform hashes. The renderer uses tracker-era resampling and an 8-bit character within 22,050 Hz PCM output; it does not use third-party recordings or claim hardware emulation.

The prompt direction is restrained canopy rain and cold mountain airflow, beneath engines, warning tones and radio. No thunder cracks, music or voices were requested. Those semantic qualities still need listening verification: numeric analysis cannot prove their absence or judge realism. The generator's `storm` role may contain role-specific texture beyond the requested wording.

Audio Studio reports no detected issues, no clipping, negligible DC offset and zero endpoint sample jump for all three loops. Its detected BPM is a generic analysis statistic, not an authored rhythm. Zero endpoint jump does not by itself prove an inaudible loop seam. The retained receipts also report slope and window-energy differences for review.

All three source candidates are now bound separately at runtime: drizzle/rain, storm and snow wind. They remain continuously clocked and crossfade by the same altitude weight used by the visual precipitation; high and orbital lanes are silent. Master and SFX settings apply after the restrained authored weather gain. Final subjective listening against propulsion, weapon fire, warning tones and radio remains pending. Cinematic environmental bridges remain a separate integration task.
