# Combat damage and counterflares

Incoming fire now carries an explicit impact class and guidance class. A successful direct missile warhead sets shield and hull to zero, including upgraded airframes. Passive shields therefore cannot turn a Sidewinder-like hit into routine chip damage. Countermeasures prevent the impact by breaking an eligible seeker before collision.

Heavy cannon rounds inflict at least 38 structural damage and lose only 25% of that value against the field; three solid hits destroy the 100-point basic hull. Lighter autocannon rounds inflict at least 28 structural damage with 35% field interaction; two hits leave the basic hull at 44 and four destroy it. Existing difficulty mitigation applies to cannon values. Structural upgrades buy limited tolerance without changing IDs, ownership, repair state or save data. Fragment and energy classes remain field-first so damage overlays, recovery and shield systems retain a purpose.

Current enemy missiles are explicitly `heat_seeking`. The live missile update now turns toward the player at its authored rate and consumes finite life. A flare only diverts nearby heat seekers: it redirects the missile toward the visible decoy, disables subsequent homing and records `countermeasure_decoyed`. Radar guidance is explicitly unaffected. The existing four-charge cassette and cooldown remain.

Telemetry now records enemy missile launches, countermeasure decoys and damage totals keyed by impact class. Sortie initialization clears those counters. This supports later vulnerable telemetry and weapon-pressure tuning.

Pinned Godot 4.6.2 passes classified impact, live runtime impact, countermeasure, player missile, service and runtime tests. The live test verifies an actual direct missile collision and a three-hit heavy-cannon destruction path. A 48-frame normal-vulnerability capture injects a heat seeker on a short controlled approach, shows full hull/shield before impact, zero/zero immediately after, the existing breakup sequence, explicit `DIRECT WARHEAD IMPACT // AIRFRAME LOST` radio status and failure debrief. It is impact-result evidence only; its artificial short approach does not prove warning fairness.

Catastrophic missiles now obey a measured launch solution. Every missile platform, including bosses, must acquire inside 430 pixels and may release only while range divided by its difficulty-scaled speed leaves at least 0.90 seconds after launch. At the Ace maximum of 161.04 pixels per second, this blocks releases inside 145 pixels. A platform that closes too far before completing its lock loses that firing opportunity instead of producing an unseen point-blank kill.

The warning is driven by the live seeker: bearing, count and time to impact progress from tracking through caution to the red danger state. `work/combat_impact_v1/native_flare_break_v2` is the accepted 48-frame native counterflare proof. An oblique seeker enters at 11 o'clock with 1.5 seconds TTI, reaches the red 0.7-second warning, and changes velocity from `(45.11, 124.05)` toward the aircraft to `(-9.60, 131.65)` toward the decoy. The HUD reports `CM 03 // MISSILE DECOYED`, telemetry records one defeated seeker, and the VX-94 exits at 100 hull / 100 shield. The review movie is `outputs/HYPERSONIC-flare-seeker-break-v2.mp4` in the shared workspace.
